Tech ethics

Ethics helps people decide what they ought to do, including when a choice is legal but may still cause harm.

Questions to ask

  • Fairness Could this system treat people differently or exclude a group?
  • Privacy Is personal information used in ways people understand and expect?
  • Safety What could go wrong, who could be harmed, and how can that risk be reduced?
  • Accountability Who is responsible for monitoring the system and correcting problems?
A responsible decision considers affected people, likely consequences, transparency, and ways to prevent or address harm.

Technology and law

Digital activities can involve many areas of law. The rules that apply depend on the facts and the jurisdiction.

Common areas to consider

  • Privacy Rules may limit how personal data is collected, protected, shared, or retained.
  • Copyright Creative works and software may be protected; permission or a license may be needed to reuse them.
  • Computer access Accessing systems or data without authorization can have serious consequences.
  • Consumer protection Claims about a product, service, or automated decision may be subject to legal requirements.
For a real legal question, consult current official guidance or a qualified legal professional in the relevant jurisdiction.
